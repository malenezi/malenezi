        Measurement blind spots

        Planning is currently done without these instruments.

          The planning implication. Targets are being met on the measures that are easy to count: seats, literacy, enrolment. The measures that show capability in work, such as placement, vacancies filled and senior roles held by nationals, are either missed or not measured, and the leader tier is at 2% of its 2030 target. Priorities below start by fixing the instruments, then shift investment to depth.
