Executive bio

Prof. Mamdouh Alenezi, Ph.D.

General Manager, SDAIA Academy Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Prof. Mamdouh Alenezi is General Manager of the SDAIA Academy at the Saudi Data & Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA), where he leads the strategy, portfolio and delivery of the national training programmes, bootcamps and professional certifications that build Saudi Arabia’s data and AI workforce. He chairs the Capacity Building & Partnerships team of the Arab Standing Committee on Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies and is vice-chair of its AI Governance team. Before SDAIA he was Director of the AI Academy and Acting Director of Technology Governance at Tahakom, and spent nearly a decade at Prince Sultan University as founding dean of the College of Space & Aviation Sciences, Dean of Quality Assurance & Development, Dean of Educational Services and Chief Information & Technology Officer. A Full Professor of Software Engineering with a Ph.D. from North Dakota State University, he has published 146 peer-reviewed works and a 2026 series of working papers on AI-native and agentic software engineering. He is a Prosci® Certified Change Practitioner.

Selected outcomes

  • A quality and performance system for a national academy — Delivery quality is assessed against published criteria rather than reputation, the portfolio is steered and reported to senior leadership through one scorecard, and the academy has a defined route to international certification.
  • Technology governance that delivery teams actually use — Enterprise-architecture maturity was raised, the governance operating model was clarified, and delivery gained speed, traceability and measurable process maturity.
  • Quality that earned certification and accreditation — The university secured ISO certifications and national and international academic accreditations.
  • One ERP across the university — Core administrative functions run on a single system, and IT planning became a standing process serving faculty, staff and students.
  • A new college and new degree programmes — founding dean of the College of Space & Aviation Sciences; designed the B.S. and M.S. in Software Engineering and the M.S. in Cybersecurity Engineering at Prince Sultan University.
  • A regional mandate — chair of the Capacity Building & Partnerships team and vice-chair of the AI Governance team, Arab Standing Committee on AI and Emerging Technologies.
