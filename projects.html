Selected cases

Six mandates, and what changed.

The short version for readers who want outcomes before detail: what I was asked to do, what I did, and what was different afterwards.

SDAIA Academy · 2026

Scaling a national academy, and proving it

Mandate
Lead Saudi Arabia’s national data and AI capability-development arm, with 70 staff across five units, towards a 2030 target of 20,000 trained specialists, and show that delivery meets a consistent, measurable standard.
What I did
Scaled delivery through a four-month cycle that trained 6,000+ specialists; redesigned measurement into 12 core KPIs and an eight-measure executive scorecard; built an instructor accreditation framework anchored to ISO 21001 and an alumni database with a verification policy.
What changed
The 6,000+ trained took the Academy’s cumulative total to 18,000, 90% of the 2030 target. The portfolio is steered through one scorecard that tracks skills, employment and professional impact, backed by 210+ verified graduate outcomes.
SDAIA Academy · Aug 2026

Two national training bodies, one operating model

Mandate
Bring the Information Technology Institute and its training programmes into the SDAIA Academy.
What I did
Led the integration in August 2026 and owned the transition communication plan, then redesigned performance measurement for the reorganised five-unit structure.
What changed
68+ programmes, 6,000+ enrolled trainees and about 50 staff now run under one operating model.
Tahakom · 2024–2026

Technology governance with externally assessed results

Mandate
As Acting Director of Technology Governance at a national smart-mobility and public-safety technology company, govern technology procurement, GRC, processes and KPIs for the company’s technology asset portfolio, including GPU infrastructure.
What I did
Directed the enterprise-architecture programme; initiated and led the CMMI programme, overhauling policies, processes and the delivery lifecycle with CI/CD-enabled DevOps; authored an enterprise PMO playbook and led governance audits across three successive cycles; supervised the consulting engagement on AI strategy.
What changed
Enterprise-architecture maturity rose from Level 2 to Level 4 on external assessment, the AI and Advanced Technologies sector was certified at CMMI Level 3, and the AI strategy, use-case portfolio and AI governance policy were approved by the CEO.
Prince Sultan University · 2020–2024

Quality that earned first-time accreditation

Mandate
As Dean of Quality Assurance & Development, own the institution-wide quality strategy.
What I did
Implemented a university-wide quality management system with assessment and evaluation frameworks across academic and administrative units, chaired the fourth strategic plan (seven years, five strategic themes) and expanded faculty development through the Teaching & Learning Center.
What changed
First-time ABET accreditation for Software Engineering, Computer Science and Information Systems; first-time HCÉRES accreditation for the Colleges of Law and Humanities; AACSB renewed for the College of Business.
Prince Sultan University · 2015–2019

One ERP across the university, in four months

Mandate
As Chief Information & Technology Officer, set and execute the university’s technology strategy.
What I did
Authored and executed a three-year digital transformation strategy; delivered an institution-wide ERP, re-engineering HR, finance, accounting and procurement processes; built an automated faculty evaluation system.
What changed
The ERP went live for 500 users in four months, core administrative functions run on a single system, and IT planning became a standing process serving faculty, staff and students.
Prince Sultan University · 2024

A college founded from zero

Mandate
As founding dean, establish the College of Space & Aviation Sciences.
What I did
Founded the college under agreements with the Saudi Space Agency and NASA, defining its academic vision, governance structure and founding portfolio of four bachelor’s and three master’s programmes.
What changed
The college has since opened.
