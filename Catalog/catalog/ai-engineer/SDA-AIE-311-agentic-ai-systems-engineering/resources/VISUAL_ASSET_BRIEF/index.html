    Visual Asset Brief¶

    SDA-AIE-311 Agentic AI Systems Engineering — production brief for design¶

    This is a commissioning document. It consolidates every "Required Visuals and Training Assets" entry across the nine module packages into one numbered list a design agency or in-house designer can quote and produce against, without needing to read the underlying course content. Assets are grouped by module in teaching order; each carries an asset ID, purpose, required elements, a style note, and the session it belongs to (Day/Hour, from course_delivery_plan.md).

    Production standards (apply to every asset)¶

    • Palette. One consistent four-to-six-colour course palette across all nine modules — do not restyle per module. Reserve one accent colour exclusively for "where agency/risk lives" call-outs (the conditional edge in M1, the trust boundary in M8, the cost bar in M9) so participants learn to recognise that accent as "pay attention here" across the whole week.
    • Bilingual labelling. Every diagram intended for print (posters) or for the participant handbook needs Arabic subtitles alongside English labels, not Arabic-only or English-only. Screenshots of terminals/traces stay in their captured language as-is.
    • Format. Diagrams as vector (SVG/PDF source, PNG export for slides). Screenshots as PNG, captured directly from the repository at data-generate-seeded state (RAFEEQ_SEED=20260311) so every cohort's designer-produced screenshot matches what a participant will actually see.
    • A2 posters. Four diagrams are also produced as A2 prints for the classroom wall (see INSTRUCTOR_DELIVERY_GUIDE.md's classroom logistics section): the agent loop (M1-D1), the MCP topology (M3-D2), the two-memory architecture (M4-D1), the supervisor topology (M6-D1), plus the layered-defence diagram (M8-D2) and the trace-tree anatomy (M9-D1) — six posters total, matching the instructor preparation checklist.
    • Numbering key. M<n>-D<n> diagram, M<n>-I<n> image/screenshot, M<n>-S<n> simulation asset, M<n>-A<n> interactive activity material, M<n>-DS<n> dataset (data asset, not a visual — listed for completeness since it feeds the visuals), M<n>-DEMO demo requirement.
