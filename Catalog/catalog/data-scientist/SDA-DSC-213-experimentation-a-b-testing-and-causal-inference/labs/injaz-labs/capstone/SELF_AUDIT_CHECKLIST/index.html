    Capstone Self-Audit Checklist¶

    SDA-DSC-213 · Injaz (إنجاز) · SDAIA Academy Complete before submission at milestone M-D (Day 4, H4 end · 13:30).

    Every item is answerable yes or no. Answer honestly — a "no" you have written down and explained in the memo costs far less than a "no" the assessor finds. Where an item does not apply to your chosen design, write n/a and one clause saying why.

    The sections match the rubric criteria and carry their weights.

    A. Experiment design & analysis plan — 15 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    A1 Is there a written design doc covering all fields of DESIGN_DOC_TEMPLATE.md?
    A2 Is the randomisation unit stated explicitly (the field the hash is computed on)?
    A3 Is the choice of that unit justified against the next-finer unit in at least two sentences?
    A4 Is exactly one OEC named as primary?
    A5 Is the OEC defined operationally (numerator, denominator, time window)?
    A6 Are there at least three guardrails, each with the direction that would alarm you?
    A7 Is the trigger defined operationally, and is the analysis population the triggered one?
    A8 Is the unit of analysis stated, and does it match the randomisation unit (or is clustering used)?
    A9 Is the estimand named (ATE / ATT / LATE / CATE), with the population and window?
    A10 Was freeze_plan() run before any cell that computes an outcome?
    A11 Is the frozen plan_hash recorded in the design doc and reproduced in the memo?
    A12 Does the notebook's freeze cell appear at a lower cell number than the first results cell?
    A13 Is the SUTVA / interference audit present with two plausible violations and the design change each implies?
    A14 If anything in the plan changed after the freeze, is it recorded in the amendments table with its effect on the conclusion?
    A15 Can you say "no metric in this analysis was chosen after I saw an outcome" without qualification?

    B. Power & sample sizing — 15 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    B1 Is a baseline rate stated, taken from the pre-period rather than from the running experiment?
    B2 Is the MDE stated as an absolute effect, in the same units as the OEC?
    B3 Is the required per-arm sample size computed and shown?
    B4 Is the arm multiplier applied (total traffic = n per arm × number of arms)?
    B5 Is the trigger rate applied to daily traffic (daily usable = eligible × trigger)?
    B6 Is the run length rounded up to whole weeks, with a sentence on why weekday and weekend users differ?
    B7 Is the relationship between MDE and n stated or shown (halving the MDE roughly quadruples n)?
    B8 Is a CUPED alternative computed with a pre-treatment covariate?
    B9 Is the variance reduction from CUPED quantified as a percentage?
    B10 Is the CUPED saving expressed in days or in sample, not just as a percentage?
    B11 If the observed run is shorter than the plan required, is the under-run named and its consequence stated?
    B12 Is the MDE compared to the practical threshold, with a sentence on what a null result could and could not rule out?

    C. Experiment integrity — 10 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    C1 Is assignment deterministic (hash + fixed salt), so the same id always maps to the same arm?
    C2 Is the salt string recorded in the design doc?
    C3 Was an SRM check run, with the chi-square statistic and p-value printed?
    C4 Is the SRM verdict stated in words ("passes / experiment invalid"), not just as a number?
    C5 Is a covariate-balance table printed, with SMD per covariate (categoricals expanded per level)?
    C6 Is the worst |SMD| reported and compared to the 0.1 threshold?
    C7 If a covariate is imbalanced, is the response adjust in analysis rather than re-randomise?
    C8 Can you confirm you did not re-randomise or re-roll any assignment?
    C9 If any dataset you analysed fails SRM, is it reported as invalid and not analysed for effect?
    C10 Is the trigger rate reported, and does the triggered sample size match what the run length predicted?

    D. A/B analysis correctness — 20 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    D1 Is the effect estimated on triggered users, not on all assigned users?
    D2 If an all-assigned number also appears, is it labelled as intention-to-treat for a different question?
    D3 Is the variance robust (HC3) or cluster-robust, not the default OLS standard error?
    D4 If the analysis rows are finer than the randomisation unit (e.g. sessions), are standard errors clustered on that unit?
    D5 Is a 95% confidence interval reported alongside every point estimate?
    D6 Is CUPED applied with a covariate that is unambiguously pre-treatment?
    D7 Did the CUPED interval get narrower (if it widened, was the cause diagnosed)?
    D8 Are the guardrails tested as a family, with the correction named (Bonferroni or equivalent) and m stated?
    D9 Is any segment sweep corrected with Benjamini–Hochberg, and are survivors labelled as hypotheses for a future test rather than findings?
    D10 Are no post-treatment variables included as covariates anywhere in the A/B analysis?
    D11 Is the effect compared to the practical threshold, not only to zero?
    D12 Does the write-up contain both verdicts explicitly: statistical ("is it there") and practical ("is it big enough")?
    D13 Was the analysis run once at the frozen horizon, with no stopping at first significance?
    D14 Are percentages and percentage points used correctly and distinguished in every sentence?

    E. Causal evaluation — 20 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    E1 Is the observational question stated with its own estimand?
    E2 Is there a DAG (drawn or written as an edge list), with each arrow justified in one line?
    E3 Is the identifying assumption written out as a single sentence you could say out loud?
    E4 Is the adjustment set derived from the DAG (backdoor criterion), not chosen by convenience?
    E5 Have you confirmed that no mediator is in the adjustment set?
    E6 Have you confirmed that no collider and no descendant of the treatment is in the adjustment set?
    E7 DiD path: is an event study plotted, with pre-activation coefficients shown?
    E8 DiD path: are the pre-period coefficients assessed in words — flat and supporting parallel trends, or not flat and therefore not identified?
    E9 DiD path: are standard errors clustered on the panel unit?
    E10 DiD path: is the staggered-rollout caveat acknowledged (TWFE weights, ramp-in attenuation)?
    E11 Matching path: is propensity overlap / common support shown and any trimming reported?
    E12 Matching path: is a post-matching balance table shown with all |SMD| < 0.1?
    E13 Matching path: are unmatched treated units counted, and is the estimand restated if many were dropped?
    E14 Matching path: is a sensitivity analysis (e.g. Rosenbaum bounds) reported with the Γ at which the conclusion flips?
    E15 Are at least two refutation or validation checks run, and were they named in the plan before they were run?
    E16 Is the result of each refutation stated, including any that did not pass?
    E17 Is the single unmeasured variable that would break the estimate named?
    E18 If the estimate is not identified, is that reported as the finding rather than worked around?
    E19 If the experimental and observational estimates disagree, is the gap explained through a specific assumption rather than one number being dropped?

    F. Decision communication — 15 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    F1 Is the memo one page?
    F2 Is there a recommendation per intervention in the first five lines?
    F3 Is each recommendation one of ship / scale / hold-and-extend / stop, stated as a verb?
    F4 Are effects reported in business units (percentage points, and completions per month where useful)?
    F5 Is a confidence interval reported for every effect, and translated into business units too?
    F6 Are there no p-values in the body of the memo (validity checks such as SRM excepted)?
    F7 Is the practical threshold stated, and is the interval compared to it explicitly?
    F8 Are the key assumptions stated as risks, with consequences?
    F9 Is there an explicit "what would change this" with a specific, costed next step?
    F10 Does the decision plot show the effects, their intervals and the threshold line?
    F11 Is the plan hash in the memo header?
    F12 Would a non-technical reader get the recommendation and its main caveat from the first paragraph alone?
    F13 Do the caveats run in both directions, rather than all conveniently supporting one conclusion?
    F14 Is any null result phrased as "not distinguishable from zero at this sample" rather than "there was no effect"?

    G. Reproducibility & integrity — 5 points¶

    # Question Y / N
    G1 Does the notebook run top to bottom on a clean kernel without errors?
    G2 Is a random seed fixed and set once, near the top?
    G3 Are all data paths relative, so the notebook runs on another machine?
    G4 Are outputs present in the submitted notebook (not cleared)?
    G5 Does the plan hash in the notebook match the plan hash in the memo?
    G6 Is every optional-dependency cell (DoWhy / EconML) either runnable or clearly marked optional with a core-stack equivalent beside it?
    G7 Is the analysis free of any peeking-driven stopping decision?
    G8 Is all work your own, with reuse limited to causal_utils and your own Lab 1–7 code?

    H. Extension — up to +5 bonus (only if mandatory scope ≥ 80)¶

    # Question Y / N
    H1 Is at least one extension from the brief attempted?
    H2 Is the extension's result reported with uncertainty, not as a point estimate?
    H3 CATE: is the multiple-testing problem across subgroups addressed explicitly?
    H4 Sequential: is the always-valid bound contrasted with the fixed-horizon interval?
    H5 Staggered DiD: is the cohort-aware estimate compared with naive TWFE, with the difference explained?
    H6 IV: are all three conditions argued and the first-stage F reported (with F < 10 flagged as unreliable)?
    H7 Power study: is the saving expressed in run days for a future Injaz experiment?
    H8 Does the extension change or confirm the recommendation, and does the memo say which?

    Final gate — the four questions asked live¶

    You will be asked these in your 6-minute briefing. If you cannot answer any one of them in a sentence, go back before you submit.

    # Question Y / N — can I answer this in one sentence?
    1 What is the identifying assumption behind your causal estimate?
    2 What single unmeasured variable would break it?
    3 What would change your recommendation, and what would it cost to find out?
    4 Which number in your memo are you least confident about, and why?

    Count your "no"s¶

    • Zero "no"s in sections C, D and E is the bar for the top band on those criteria.
    • Any "no" in section A10–A12 (the freeze) caps criterion 1 and endangers criterion 7.
    • Any "no" that you keep must appear in the memo as a stated limitation. A declared weakness is a scored strength; an undeclared one is an anti-pattern flag.
