    Injaz (إنجاز) Course Datasets — Data Dictionary¶

    SDA-DSC-213 · Experimentation, A/B Testing and Causal Inference · SDAIA Academy

    All data is synthetic, generated by make_data.py. No real citizen or government data is used anywhere in this course. Ground-truth effects are planted so every method can be checked against a number that was deliberately put there — see GROUND_TRUTH.json.

    Regenerate everything with:

    cd data && python make_data.py

    The scenario¶

    Injaz is a unified Saudi digital-government services platform: citizens renew licences, issue certificates, update addresses and pay fees online. Three threads run through the whole course:

    Thread Question Method Days
    Guided uploader Does a redesigned document-upload step raise service completion? randomised A/B test 1–2
    Regional reminders Did the region-by-region reminder rollout raise completion? difference-in-differences 3
    Digital-literacy training Did the voluntary training programme help those who took it? matching / IPW 3
    AI autofill Should we launch autofill nationally? capstone A/B + causal evaluation 4

    Files¶

    simulated_potential_outcomes.parquet — 20,000 rows · Module 1¶

    The only file in the course containing both potential outcomes for every unit. This is impossible in reality, which is precisely why the course starts here: you can watch an estimator succeed or fail against a truth you can see.

    Column Type Meaning
    user_id int Unit identifier
    region, age, device, device_age_years, distance_km, prior_sessions, prior_completion_rate, digital_literacy_score, signup_cohort mixed Baseline covariates
    latent_literacy float The true confounder (exposed only in this teaching file)
    y0 0/1 Y(0) — completion if not given the uploader
    y1 0/1 Y(1) — completion if given the uploader
    tau_individual int Y(1) − Y(0), the individual effect. Never observable in practice
    treated_self_selected 0/1 Opt-in assignment (confounded by literacy)
    treated_randomised 0/1 Coin-flip assignment
    y_observed_self_selected 0/1 What you would actually see under opt-in
    y_observed_randomised 0/1 What you would actually see under randomisation
    p_select float P(opt in), the selection mechanism

    Planted truth: ATE = +5.04 pp. Naive self-selected comparison = +19.30 pp (selection bias ≈ +14.32 pp). Naive randomised comparison ≈ +5.04 pp.

    injaz_users_pool.csv — 200,000 rows · Module 2¶

    The eligible user pool for assignment, SRM and balance work. No outcomes — this file is used before an experiment runs.

    Key columns: user_id, region, age, device, os_family, device_age_years, distance_km, prior_sessions, prior_completion_rate, digital_literacy_score, signup_cohort, preferred_language, is_eligible.

    injaz_users.csv — 20,000 rows · Modules 5 & 6¶

    The observational analysis cohort. Carries three separate causal stories:

    Column Meaning
    enrolled_training 0/1 — self-selected into the digital-literacy programme
    online_filing 0/1 — endogenous choice of filing channel
    log_distance_km log km to the nearest service centre — the instrument for online_filing
    support_calls count — a mediator/collider trap: downstream of both treatment and outcome
    completed 0/1 — the outcome

    Planted truths: ATT of training = +6.0 pp (naive difference ≈ +11.5 pp). LATE of online filing = +8.0 pp (OLS biased to ≈ +10.9 pp; first-stage F ≈ 2,100).

    Use log_distance_km, not distance_km, as the instrument. Raw kilometres are heavily right-skewed and the first stage is mis-specified in levels — a useful five-minute exercise in its own right.

    injaz_experiment_results.csv — 80,000 rows · Modules 2 & 4¶

    The guided-uploader randomised experiment, user-level.

    Column Meaning
    variant control / treatment — the randomised arm
    triggered 0/1 — did the user reach the document-upload step at all?
    exposed 0/1 — treatment and triggered
    completed 0/1 — the OEC
    support_contact, session_duration_sec, error_flag, drop_off the guardrail family
    prior_completion_rate pre-experiment covariate — the CUPED variable
    region, device, age_band, signup_cohort segmentation

    Planted truth: +2.2 pp on completion among triggered users. Analysing all 80,000 assigned users instead of the 49,653 triggered ones dilutes the effect by roughly the trigger rate (+2.16 pp becomes +1.34 pp) — a deliberate trap.

    injaz_experiment_results_srm_broken.csv — 78,747 rows¶

    The same experiment with ~3% of the treatment arm silently dropped. SRM chi-square ≈ 16.5, p ≈ 4.8 × 10⁻⁵ → the experiment is invalid and must not be analysed. Used for the SRM injector demo.

    injaz_pre_period.csv — 50,000 rows · Module 3¶

    Pre-experiment window for CUPED: user_id, pre_sessions, pre_completion_rate, pre_avg_duration_sec.

    Planted truth: corr(pre_completion_rate, post-period completed) ≈ 0.37 → CUPED variance reduction ≈ 13.5%, i.e. ~1.16× the effective sample for free.

    injaz_traffic_forecast.csv — 90 rows · Module 3¶

    Daily eligible traffic for run-length planning: date, day_of_week, eligible_users, trigger_rate, is_weekend. Follows the KSA working week (Fri–Sat are ~58% of a weekday), which is exactly why run lengths must be whole weeks.

    injaz_sessions.csv.gz — 339,017 rows · Modules 2 & 4¶

    Session-level events, ~4.2 sessions per user. Outcomes are correlated within user, so a session-level analysis of a user-randomised experiment produces standard errors that are far too small. That is the point of the file.

    Columns: session_id, user_id, session_start, region, device, service_type, variant, triggered_uploader, exposed_uploader, session_completed, duration_sec.

    triggered_uploader = the session reached the document-upload step, and it is present in both arms (about 49.7% of control sessions and 49.6% of treatment sessions). exposed_uploader = reached that step and was in the treatment arm. Filtering the clustering demo on triggered_uploader is therefore legitimate: both arms survive the filter.

    injaz_regions_panel.csv — 312 rows (13 regions × 24 months) · Module 5¶

    The staggered reminder rollout.

    Column Meaning
    activation_month period the region switched on (blank = never treated)
    treated_ever, post, treated_post DiD indicators
    months_since_activation relative time for the event study
    completion_rate, completions, eligible_users, reminders_sent outcomes and volume

    Activation waves: periods 10, 13, 16, 19; 4 regions never treated.

    Planted truth: full ATT = +3.0 pp, with a two-month ramp-in (60% of the effect in the first two months). TWFE therefore recovers ≈ +2.6 pp — the attenuation is real, not a bug, and is the opening for the staggered-DiD extension in the capstone.

    injaz_regions_panel_pretrend.csv — 312 rows¶

    Identical, except treated regions are given a divergent pre-trend. The event study shows non-zero pre-period coefficients and parallel trends fails. Used for the parallel-trends-breaker simulation.

    collider_sim.parquet — 50,000 rows · Module 6¶

    Two independent causes (digital_literacy, service_simplicity) of one collider (completed).

    Planted truth: unconditional correlation ≈ 0.00; conditional on completed == 1 it becomes ≈ −0.20. Nothing changed in the world — only the rows you looked at.

    injaz_autofill_ab.csv — 70,000 rows · Capstone¶

    The AI-assisted autofill experiment. Same structure as the uploader experiment.

    Planted truth: +1.4 pp on completion among triggered users (49,510 of the 70,000 assigned trigger, a rate of 70.7%), against a 1.0 pp practical-significance threshold. Analysing all 70,000 assigned users instead dilutes +1.37 pp to +0.97 pp — the trigger rate times the planted lift. The confidence interval is designed to straddle that threshold: the effect is real and statistically significant, yet the data cannot establish that it is large enough to justify the build. The correct capstone answer is a hold / extend, not a ship — and a participant who writes "p < 0.05, ship it" has missed the entire course.

    Ground truth summary¶

    Quantity Planted Typically recovered
    Uploader ATE (simulator) +5.04 pp +5.04 pp randomised · +19.30 pp self-selected
    Uploader A/B lift (triggered) +2.2 pp +2.16 pp
    Training ATT +6.0 pp +6.5 pp matched · +6.6 pp IPW · +11.5 pp naive
    Reminder ATT (full) +3.0 pp +2.6 pp TWFE (ramp-in attenuation)
    Online-filing LATE +8.0 pp +7.8 pp 2SLS · +10.9 pp OLS
    Autofill lift +1.4 pp +1.37 pp, CI straddles the 1.0 pp threshold
    CUPED variance reduction — ~13.5%
    Peeking FPR at 20 looks — ~25% (nominal 5%)
