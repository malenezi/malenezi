    SDA-DSC-112 — Complete dataset package¶

    Data Visualization and Storytelling · SDAIA Academy · Data Scientist Track¶

    Every dataset required to deliver the three-day programme end to end: lectures, live demos, six hands-on labs, five case studies, knowledge checks, benchmark exercises, dashboards and the graded capstone.

    Golden thread. All 30 datasets describe one artefact — the Tayseer (تيسير) National Digital Services Programme, a Vision 2030 initiative moving government service delivery from branches to digital channels. Nothing here is a throwaway example; every lab evolves the same story.

    Coverage. July 2021 – June 2026 (60 months) · 13 administrative regions · 9 service categories · 4 delivery channels · 12.5 million transactions in the latest month.

    All data is synthetic. Volumes, costs, names, emails and comments are generated. No real individual, entity or transaction is represented. Figures are calibrated to be plausible for a Saudi national service programme, not to reproduce any actual published statistic.

    1. The story the data tells¶

    This spine is deliberate — instructors should know it before Day 1, because every lab converges on it.
    Situation National digital adoption has risen from 33.9% to 63.8%; cost per transaction fell 34% and satisfaction rose 12%.
    Complication Five regions — Jazan, Najran, Al-Baha, Asir, Northern Borders — have stalled (under 0.08pp per month for nine months). The same five exceed the 18 SAR cost-per-transaction target. Left alone they finish December roughly 8pp short.
    The trap AVERAGE(digital_adoption_pct) returns 61.2%; the correct user-weighted recomputation returns 63.8%. The 2.6-point averages-of-averages bug is reproducible from the shipped data — Module 4 depends on it.
    The objection "Those regions are just small." The rebuttal is in the data: 15.8% of the population, digital transactions per capita 12% below national, and the highest unit cost in the country (24.6 vs 14.6 SAR).
    Evidence Support tickets show authentication failures at 27% of ticket volume in the five regions versus 17% elsewhere — a scoped, fixable root cause.
    The ask Where should the next SAR 40 million go? Three options are costed. Only the targeted regional option lifts stalled regions to 65% by December (four of the five; Jazan lands at 63.9%); the platform rebuild has the higher ceiling but lands eight months late.

    5. The measure rules (read before building anything)¶

    Rule Columns Consequence
    Additive transactions, complaints Safe to SUM at any grain
    Semi-additive unique_users Sum within a month, never across months
    Non-additive digital_adoption_pct, cost_per_txn_sar, csat, avg_completion_min, sla_met_pct Never AVERAGE — recompute from components 
    import pandas as pd, numpy as np
df = pd.read_csv("data/core/tayseer_services.csv", parse_dates=["month"])

TARGET = 65.0
def national_adoption(frame):                     # percentages are NON-additive
    return frame.groupby("month").apply(
        lambda x: 100 * (x.digital_adoption_pct / 100 * x.unique_users).sum()
                  / x.unique_users.sum())

latest = df[df.month == df.month.max()]
print(round(latest.digital_adoption_pct.mean(), 1))      # 61.2  <- the bug
print(round(national_adoption(df).iloc[-1], 1))          # 63.8  <- correct

    digital_adoption_pct is a segment-level measure at month × region × service_category, repeated across that segment's four channel rows. That is exactly why the naive average is wrong and why weighting by unique_users is right — the teaching point is built into the grain.

    6. Integrity guarantees¶

    docs/validation_report.md records 104 automated checks in two families — 33 on the core datasets and 71 on the Case B and Case C extensions — all passing. Among them:

    • The fact grain is unique and complete (60 × 13 × 9 × 4 = 28,080 rows), with no nulls and no orphan keys
    • Weighted national adoption 63.8% vs naive 61.2% — the deck's figures, reproducible
    • Exactly five regions above the 18 SAR cost target, and they are the same five that have stalled on adoption
    • Cost per transaction −34% and CSAT +12% across the window
    • Transaction durations reproduce fact completion times (r = 0.997); survey CSAT tracks fact CSAT by region (r = 0.93)
    • Each investment option allocates exactly SAR 40M; only the targeted option moves any stalled region to the target — four of the five, with Jazan still short at 63.9%
    • Per-capita rates in regional_equity_latest reconcile exactly with regional_scorecard_latest, and both recompute from the fact table
    • Lorenz cumulative shares close at exactly 1.0; the Gini is 0.0372, recomputed from the shipped segment areas
    • Jazan is 7th of 13 by raw digital volume and 13th of 13 per capita — the rank shift a raw-count choropleth hides
    • Every one of the 26 figures in kpi_spin_pairs is re-executed from the source CSVs, including the 36.8 lie factor

    7. Bilingual and accessibility notes¶

    Column names and keys are English throughout, per the course's delivery standard. Arabic labels ship as values — region_ar, category_ar, service_name_ar, comment_ar, month_name_ar — so Module 3's Arabic RTL rendering exercise works without translating the schema. Files are UTF-8 with BOM for clean opening in Excel and Power BI on Windows. brand_palette.csv carries CVD-safety and contrast columns so the colour-blind simulation step has ground truth to check against.
