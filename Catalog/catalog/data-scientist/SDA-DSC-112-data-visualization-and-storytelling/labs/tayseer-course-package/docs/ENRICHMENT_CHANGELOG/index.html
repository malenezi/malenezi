    Enrichment Changelog¶

    سجل الإضافات¶

    What was added to the SDA-DSC-112 package in this revision, and why — SDAIA Academy, Data Scientist track.

    Revision date: 14 September 2026.

    Why this revision exists¶

    The delivered package taught the craft correctly and taught it entirely on synthetic data. Two things were missing. There was no critique canon — no set of real, famous, flawed visualisations to take apart, so participants could learn to judge rather than only to copy. And there was no applied ethics thread tied to reproducible numbers, so "be honest" remained an exhortation rather than a method.

    This revision adds both, plus the Saudi public-source guidance participants need the week after the course ends. Thirty-one slides, five datasets, twelve executed lab notebooks, twelve figures and three documents. Every number added is reproducible from the shipped data; scripts/validate_extensions.py re-executes each stated operation rather than trusting the build, and docs/validation_report.md records 104 of 104 checks passing — 33 on the core datasets, 71 on the extensions.

    Nothing was removed. The deck goes from 150 slides to 181; the dataset package from 25 files to 30.

    2. Datasets — 5 new files¶

    The package goes from 25 to 30 datasets. All five are generated by scripts/build_extensions.py from the core data — no figure in them is hand-typed — and verified by scripts/validate_extensions.py, which re-executes every stated operation against the shipped CSVs.

    File Rows × cols What it is Why it was added
    data/supporting/regional_equity_latest.csv 13 × 28 The thirteen regions normalised: per-capita rates, index against the national figure, and the two rankings a raw-count map and a rate map produce Case C. The rank shift is the argument. Jazan is 7th of 13 by raw digital volume and 13th per capita — a six-place fall a choropleth hides
    data/supporting/regional_equity_lorenz.csv 13 Regions sorted by digital transactions per capita, with cumulative population and transaction shares The Lorenz curve and its Gini of 0.0372. Deliberately anticlimactic — the inequality is in the level of adoption, not the concentration of volume, and noticing that is the chart-selection lesson
    data/instructional/kpi_spin_pairs.csv 13 × 15 Thirteen ways to spin a KPI, each with a spun and an honest figure, the exact operation that produces it, severity and detectability Case B. All 26 figures re-execute from the source CSVs, so every accusation is checkable rather than rhetorical
    data/instructional/success_dashboard_spec.csv 20 × 14 Ten dashboard tiles, twice: a spun variant and an honest one over identical underlying truth The tile-by-tile pairing behind the Case B redesign. Titles are identical across variants — same truth, two treatments
    data/instructional/critique_canon.csv 10 × 12 The ten canonical works: encodings, the claim, what it does brilliantly, the documented flaw, the transferable Tayseer lesson and two critique questions each The answer key behind every canon slide, and the source for the Canon Quickfire cards

    Three results from these files that are quotable on a slide, all recomputed in validation: the 36.8 Tufte lie factor on the truncated-axis pair (a 3.50% real change drawn as a 128.71% one); the averages-of-averages gap — 61.19% naive against 63.84% user-weighted, recorded as 2.65 pp in kpi_spin_pairs.csv and 2.64 pp at full precision in the core checks; and the Gini of 0.0372 that refuses to be dramatic.

    3. Lab notebooks — 12 files¶

    Six labs, each as a starter and a solution. All twelve execute clean end to end; the full set runs in under two minutes headless on a two-core container. Every published figure below is asserted in a notebook cell, so a change to the data breaks the run rather than silently changing a claim.

    Lab Notebooks Class minutes Runtime Produces
    1 · Perception lab1_perception_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 50 ~10 s 4 figures
    2 · Chart selection lab2_chart_selection_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 50 ~15 s 6 figures + 1 interactive HTML
    3 · Declutter & RTL lab3_declutter_redesign_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 50 ~15 s 7 figures
    4 · Dashboard lab4_dashboard_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 100 (4a + 4b) ~15 s 4 figures + dashboard HTML + app/dashboard.py + lab4_bi_measures.md
    5 · Storyboard lab5_storyboard_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 50 ~10 s 8 figures + BLUF memo + story/
    6 · Delivery clinic lab6_delivery_clinic_starter.ipynb · _solution.ipynb 50 ~10 s 1 figure + story/

    Shared infrastructure added alongside them: tayseer_viz.py — the Okabe–Ito palette, the decluttered matplotlib theme, the correctly-implemented non-additive measures, one principle-compliant chart function, and the Arabic reshaping and bidi helpers. It refuses to roll up a non-additive column without its declared weight, which is the single error the course exists to prevent.

    One ordering constraint. Lab 6 imports story/tayseer_storyboard.py, which Lab 5 writes. Run Lab 5 before Lab 6. Labs 1–4 are independent of each other.

    6. Known limitations and open items¶

    Recorded honestly so that the next instructor inherits the problems along with the material.

    Licensing and access constraints¶

    Item Consequence
    The FT Visual Vocabulary is not open-licensed — © The Financial Times Limited, all rights reserved It can only be linked, never reproduced in slides or handouts without clearance from FT syndication. Deck slide 59 teaches the nine categories in the course's own words and links to the repository. The EU Publications Office maintains a separately-licensed derivative, which is the one to put in a printed pack
    The OWID Grapher source code is no longer MIT The current licence permits viewing and studying the code only. Do not tell a class they can fork it. The date the licence changed is unverified — say "the current licence", not a year. Wikipedia and most secondary sources are out of date
    NYT COVID data is CC BY-NC 4.0 Noncommercial, attribution required. The dataset is archived — it stopped updating 24 March 2023, and the excess-deaths files on 18 January 2021
    Several Saudi government sites block automated access dga.gov.sa, sdaia.gov.sa and parts of my.gov.sa return 403 or a WAF block to scripts. All *.data.gov.sa hosts were unreachable from the research environment — an egress restriction, not evidence about the portal. No scraping exercise was written against any of them, by design; the three worked exercises in the sources guide use browser retrieval and direct PDF download only
    FiveThirtyEight's archive is gone from its URLs ABC redirected the archive on 15 May 2026; every fivethirtyeight.com/features/… URL now 302s to abcnews.com/politics. Every 538 reference in the deck and in the pack routes through web.archive.org. Re-check before each delivery
    DGA, Vision 2030 and DataSaudi state no reuse licence All three are cite-only in the guide. Absence of a stated licence is not permission

    Historical claims that are hedged rather than asserted¶

    The research file carries an explicit UNVERIFIED / DISPUTED section, and fifteen items in it shaped what the slides and the pack are allowed to say. The ones that most affect delivery:

    Claim How it is handled
    Snow's "83 deaths in the week ending 2 September", "535 workhouse inmates, 5 deaths", "70 brewery workers" Omitted entirely. Widely repeated, not verified against Snow's own text. The workhouse well and the brewery beer allowance are stated without numbers
    Whether Snow's walking-distance map appeared in the second edition of his book Not asserted. Sources differ on whether the 1855 vestry report is dated 1854 or 1855
    Nightingale's rose causing specific reforms Hedged. The diagram was the persuasive centrepiece of her lobbying; no primary document attributing a reform to it was verified, and Hugh Small argues her Lines diagram was probably more influential
    Minard's troop figures Cited as "the standard tabulated transcription", not as manuscript figures. The 422,000 → 10,000 arithmetic was verified against that transcription
    Romano et al. (2020) effect sizes The citation, journal, volume, pages, DOI and verbatim conclusions are used; no sample size or percentage-point difference is quoted — they were not retrievable
    The FT trajectory chart's 11 March 2020 launch Stated as "on Burn-Murdoch's own account"; not verified against the original FT page or tweet timestamp
    FiveThirtyEight's 2020 final probability Not stated anywhere. The widely repeated "Biden 89 in 100" could not be verified from a live source
    A FiveThirtyEight critique of the hurricane cone of uncertainty Does not exist as far as could be verified. The critique is attributed to Broad et al. (BAMS, 2007) and to Alberto Cairo
    Awards for "How the Virus Got Out" Not claimed. The 2021 Pulitzer went to the Times' pandemic coverage as a whole, and the citation does not name graphics
    The Trendalyzer sale price Not stated. The March 2007 Google acquisition is confirmed; terms were not disclosed
    The 10th-anniversary edition of Storytelling with Data ISBN, date, publisher and page count confirmed; its chapter list is unverified. Say "six lessons, ten chapters", which holds for the 2015 edition

    Deviations from the INSTRUCTOR_PACKAGE, as flagged in the notebooks README¶

    Five, all deliberate, all flagged in the notebooks where they occur.

    # Deviation Resolution
    1 bad_dashboard_m1.png (Lab 1) and cluttered_m3.png (Lab 3) are not shipped with the data package Both are built in code inside the notebook, which makes the critique target reproducible and lets participants see each violation in the source
    2 Several expected-output blocks say three regions are below target The data says nine are below target, five of those are stalled (Tier 1), and three are more than 9 pp below. The notebooks use all three counts and name which is which; the story's ask is about the five stalled regions
    3 Module 3's code example annotates a "March 2024 service outage" behind Najran's plateau No such step change exists in tayseer_services.csv. Najran rises smoothly and flattens over the last nine months; Lab 3 annotates the nine-month stall instead
    4 Module 2's expected output says national adoption goes 41% → 68% and crosses 65% in month 21 The data says 34.2% → 63.8%, never crossing. The notebooks use the data; the gap to target is the whole point of the story
    5 Lab 2 is specified with four questions lab2_questions.csv ships five — Q5 is the thirteen-overlapping-lines / small-multiples question. Lab 2 covers all five

    Open items for the next instructor¶

    # Item What to do
    1 Two new activities reference notebook sections that do not yet exist. Slide 69 points at "lab2_chart_selection_starter.ipynb, section 2b" and slide 109 at "lab4_dashboard_starter.ipynb, section 4b-alt". Neither section is present in the shipped starters: Lab 2 contains no per-capita, rank-shift or Lorenz work, and Lab 4 contains no animation-versus-small-multiples build Either add the two sections to the starter and solution notebooks, or run both activities from regional_equity_latest.csv, regional_equity_lorenz.csv and the run sheets in CRITIQUE_ACTIVITY_PACK.md. The data and the facilitator script are complete; only the notebook scaffolding is missing
    2 critique_canon.csv's module_used_in column disagrees with the deck's placement for four rows — CAN-02 (Minard: CSV M3, deck M1), CAN-04 (Gapminder: CSV M5, deck M4), CAN-05 (OWID: CSV M4, deck M3) and CAN-08, which the CSV keeps as one row and the deck splits across M3 and M5 The deck is the authority for where a work is taught; the CSV for what is said about it. Reconcile the column, or leave it and note the split — the pack documents it either way
    3 Deck slide 20 says "nine traps"; the sources guide carries ten verified gotchas plus one instructor-judgement item The deck's nine are a subset chosen for the slide. The two it omits — several stats.gov.sa deep links 404, and the disputed SDAIA-versus-DGA ownership of the national portal — are in the guide. The "percentages do not travel" bullet on the slide is instructor judgement, not a verified source finding, and is marked as such in the guide
    4 The Gapminder figure uses Plotly's bundled Gapminder sample dataset, which ends in 2007 Adequate for the animation-versus-small-multiples argument, which is about form rather than currency. If a participant asks why the frame says 2007, say so plainly. Do not present it as current Gapminder Tools data
    5 Three canon figures are reconstructions, not reproductions — the needle, the 538 displays and the Incalculable Loss mock-up Each carries a footnote saying so, and the caption is on the slide. Say it out loud when the slide goes up; a reconstruction presented as a reproduction is the exact failure the course is teaching against
    6 Plotly PNG export requires a Chrome binary that is not present in every training environment and cannot always be downloaded The notebooks always write the interactive HTML, which is the real deliverable, and always render a matplotlib mirror as a PNG. Nothing in out/ is missing as a result
    7 Arabic rendering depends on an installed font. setup_arabic() looks for Amiri, then Scheherazade, then KacstOne, then FreeSerif, and returns False if none is present or if arabic-reshaper / python-bidi are missing Install with apt-get install -y fonts-hosny-amiri and verify with fc-list :lang=ar. Check this on the room's actual machines the week before. If it fails, fall back to English labels and say so on the slide — shipping tofu boxes or disconnected letters in front of a room that reads Arabic natively costs the instructor the room
    8 Whether the Red Team activity runs on Day 2, or is dropped to protect Lab 4b, is left as an explicit instructor-judgement call on the pre-flight slide Red Team is the better predictor of Day 3 performance. Protect it if you can. The compressed-delivery table in CRITIQUE_ACTIVITY_PACK.md gives the cut order if you cannot
    9 Every public URL in the front matter and in the sources guide needs re-verification before each delivery Saudi government web estate is consolidating actively — 236 sites closed in 2024. The re-verification checklist at the end of PUBLIC_DATA_SOURCES_GUIDE.md is the list to work through
    10 Deck slide 19 states download formats and developer APIs for the national open data portal that the research could not verify first-hand — all *.data.gov.sa hosts were unreachable Either verify on-network and keep the row, or reduce it to what is confirmed: the portal exists, it is linked from the GASTAT footer, and its licence is an attribution-plus-disclosure open licence whose exact name is unverified. The sources guide already takes the reduced position
