    Data Dictionary — SDA-DSC-112 Tayseer dataset family¶

    Programme: Data Visualization and Storytelling (SDA-DSC-112) · SDAIA Academy
    Golden thread: the Tayseer (تيسير) National Digital Services Programme
    Coverage: July 2021 – June 2026 (60 months) · 13 administrative regions · 9 service categories · 4 channels
    All data is synthetic. No real individual, entity or transaction is represented.

    1. Grain and joins¶

    dim_calendar (month) ─┐
dim_regions  (region) ─┼─→ tayseer_services  [month × region × service_category × channel]
dim_services (category)┤        ↑ 28,080 rows, the single fact table
dim_channels (channel) ┘

    Every other dataset joins on region, month, service_category, channel or service_id.

    2. The three measure rules¶

    Rule Applies to Consequence
    Additive transactions, complaints Safe to SUM at any grain
    Semi-additive unique_users Sum within a month, never across months
    Non-additive digital_adoption_pct, cost_per_txn_sar, csat, avg_completion_min, sla_met_pct Never AVERAGE — recompute from components

    The deliberate teaching artefact: for the latest month, AVERAGE(digital_adoption_pct) returns 61.2% while the correct user-weighted recomputation returns 63.8%. The 2.6-point gap is the averages-of-averages bug, and it is reproducible from the shipped data.

    3. Seasonality built into the data¶

    Pattern Months Effect
    Ramadan Feb–Apr (shifts each year) Overall volume −14%, branch volume −28%
    Hajj May–Jul (shifts each year) Makkah and Madinah +34%
    School enrolment August–September Education & Training +48%
    Year-end renewals December Traffic & Transport +27%
    Summer lull July–August Most categories −7%

    4. Reference values at the latest month (June 2026)¶

    Measure Value Target
    National digital adoption (recomputed) 63.8% 65%
    National digital adoption (naive average) 61.2% — (wrong method)
    National cost per transaction 16.94 SAR 18 SAR
    Regions above the 18 SAR cost target 5 0
    National CSAT 4.16 4.30
    Monthly transactions 12.5 million —
    Five-year change CSAT +12%, cost per transaction −34% —

    5. Column reference¶

    data/core/tayseer_services.csv¶

    # Column Type Description Notes
    1 month str Reporting month, first day of month (YYYY-MM-01) Join key to dim_calendar
    2 region str Administrative region (English) Join key to dim_regions.region
    3 service_category str Service family Join key to dim_services.service_category
    4 channel str Delivery channel Join key to dim_channels.channel
    5 transactions integer Completed service transactions in the segment ADDITIVE - safe to SUM
    6 unique_users integer Distinct beneficiaries in the segment Additive within a month only; do not sum across months
    7 digital_adoption_pct decimal Share of the month x region x service_category segment delivered through digital channels NON-ADDITIVE - repeated on all four channel rows of a segment. Recompute as SUM(pct/100unique_users)/SUM(unique_users)100
    8 avg_completion_min decimal Mean end-to-end completion time NON-ADDITIVE - weight by transactions; long right tail at branches
    9 cost_per_txn_sar decimal Fully loaded unit cost to deliver one transaction NON-ADDITIVE - weight by transactions
    10 csat decimal Mean satisfaction score, 1-5 NON-ADDITIVE - weight by transactions
    11 sla_met_pct decimal Share of transactions inside the service-level target NON-ADDITIVE - weight by transactions
    12 complaints integer Formal complaints logged in the segment ADDITIVE - safe to SUM

    # Column Type Description Notes
    1 technique_id str Identifier, SPIN-01 to SPIN-13
    2 technique_en str Name of the spin technique (English)
    3 technique_ar str Name of the spin technique (Arabic) RTL label

    11 the_honest_statement str What the data actually supports
    12 the_fix str The design or governance change that removes the technique Annotation, weighting, denominator or threshold binding
    13 severity str Low / Medium / High How much the decision changes
    14 detectability str Easy / Medium / Hard How visible the technique is to a reader who does not have the data
    15 source_file str The shipped CSV the figures come from Always a path under data/
