    Masar data dictionary — SDA-DSC-214¶

    Canonical column definitions for every file in data/raw/ and data/fixtures/. Schemas here are authoritative and match CONVENTIONS.md. Do not add, rename or reorder columns; downstream labs, dbt models, Great Expectations suites and the capstone all bind to these names.

    How to read the PDPL column¶

    Every column carries a classification under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law. The four levels used across this course:

    class meaning default control
    public reference data with no link to an individual no restriction
    internal operational data, commercially sensitive, not personal role-based read
    personal identifies or relates to an identifiable natural person column-level access control; never in BI or the online store in raw form; in scope for erasure
    sensitive-personal personal data whose exposure creates concrete risk to the individual — here, precise location and identity hashes strict need-to-know; minimise (geohash, not lat/lon); shortest retention; in scope for erasure

    Retention is the maximum period the raw value may be kept in the lakehouse before the retention job deletes it and VACUUM makes the deletion physical. Aggressive retention protects data subjects and cuts storage; conservative retention preserves the audit trail. The tension is the point — the numbers below are the course's documented, defensible policy, not a universal answer.

    Timezone: every timestamp is stored in UTC. The business operates in Asia/Riyadh (UTC+3). Presentation converts; storage never does. Roughly 2% of trips timestamps are deliberately written as DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM instead of YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS — see README.md#the-deliberate-defects.

    Currency: SAR throughout.

    raw/trips_YYYY-MM-DD.csv¶

    One row per trip request. ~48,200 rows/day at --scale 1.0; 7,230/day in the shipped sample. CSV, header row, UTF-8, , separated, " quoted where needed.

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    trip_id string no TRP-20260601-0000000 Primary key. TRP-<YYYYMMDD>-<7-digit sequence>. Unique within the raw feed; the fixtures deliberately break that. internal 7 years (financial record)
    rider_id string no RDR-013698 Pseudonymous rider identifier. The subject key for PDPL erasure. Never emit raw into BI or the Redis online store. personal 24 months, or on erasure request
    driver_id string no DRV-02492 FK → drivers.driver_id. Identifies a natural person (a driver). personal 7 years (employment/tax)
    vehicle_id string no VEH-00884 FK → vehicles.vehicle_id. internal 7 years
    city string no DMM City code, one of RUH (Riyadh), JED (Jeddah), DMM (Dammam). The dirty_batch fixture injects out-of-domain values. public indefinite
    pickup_zone_id string no Z-DMM-10 FK → zones.zone_id. Where the trip started. internal 24 months
    dropoff_zone_id string no Z-DMM-06 FK → zones.zone_id. Requested destination — populated even for cancelled trips. internal 24 months
    pickup_ts timestamp (UTC) no 2026-06-01 19:39:00 Trip start. ~2% of rows use DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM instead (deliberate). Combined with rider_id this is behavioural data. personal 24 months
    dropoff_ts timestamp (UTC) yes 2026-06-01 19:44:12 Trip end. NULL for cancelled_rider, cancelled_driver, no_show — structurally, not as a defect. personal 24 months
    distance_km double yes 3.04 Metered distance. NULL when the trip did not complete. internal 24 months
    duration_min double yes 5.2 Metered duration. NULL when the trip did not complete. A handful of rows per day are negative (deliberate defect). Never assume dropoff_ts - pickup_ts equals this. internal 24 months
    fare_sar double no 12.01 Total charged, SAR. 0.00 or a small cancellation fee for non-completed trips. Correlated with distance, duration and surge. The currency_shift fixture silently multiplies one city's values by 100. internal 7 years (financial record)
    surge_multiplier double no 1.0 Demand multiplier, 1.0–3.0 in 0.1 steps. Rises at the morning and evening peaks, on Fridays after Jumu'ah, in bad weather (dust, sandstorm, rain, fog) and at airport pickups. internal 7 years
    payment_type string no mada One of mada, credit_card, apple_pay, stc_pay, cash, wallet. mada is the Saudi national debit network. internal 7 years
    status string no completed One of completed (93.0%), cancelled_rider (4.2%), cancelled_driver (1.9%), no_show (0.9%). Everything except completed implies NULL dropoff fields. internal 7 years
    dropoff_geohash string yes thewke6 Precision-7 geohash (~150 m cell) of the dropoff point. This is minimised precise location — Masar deliberately stores a cell, not coordinates. NULL for non-completed trips (structural) and injected NULL on 3.1% of completed trips (deliberate defect). sensitive-personal 90 days at precision 7, then truncate to precision 5

    Known correlations to preserve if you regenerate: fare_sar ~ distance_km r ≈ 0.94; fare_sar ~ duration_min r ≈ 0.98; mean implied speed drops from ~38 km/h at 03:00 local to ~21 km/h at 08:00 and 17:00–18:00 local; Friday's hourly demand curve is materially different from Sunday–Thursday's (late start, post-Jumu'ah bump, strong evening).

    raw/gps/gps_YYYY-MM-DD.ndjson[.gz]¶

    Newline-delimited JSON, one object per GPS ping. ~25 pings per completed trip; ~1,161,000 rows/day at --scale 1.0, ~175,000/day in the shipped sample. Shipped gzipped — see README.md#gps-is-gzipped.

    {"event_id":"ev_00009f025f480001","vehicle_id":"VEH-00884","driver_id":"DRV-02492",
 "trip_id":"TRP-20260601-0000000","ts":"2026-06-01T19:38:56Z",
 "payload":{"lat":26.41803,"lon":50.10770,"speed_kmh":15.3,"heading_deg":211,"accuracy_m":17.3},
 "producer_version":"2.4.0"}
    field type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    event_id string no ev_00009f025f480001 Event key for exactly-once ingest and deduplication. ~0.4% are duplicated (deliberate defect) — the stream must dedupe on this. internal 30 days raw
    vehicle_id string no VEH-00884 FK → vehicles.vehicle_id. The Kafka message key — co-partitions a vehicle's pings so per-vehicle ordering survives. internal 30 days raw
    driver_id string no DRV-02492 FK → drivers.driver_id. Makes each ping attributable to a natural person. personal 30 days raw
    trip_id string no TRP-20260601-0000000 FK → trips.trip_id. The only path from a ping to a rider — an erasure that filters on rider_id alone misses GPS entirely. internal 30 days raw
    ts timestamp (UTC, RFC3339 Z) no 2026-06-01T19:38:56Z Event time, from the device — not ingest time. ~1% arrive out of order (deliberate defect); the late_pings fixture adds events that are minutes late. personal 30 days raw
    payload.lat double no 26.41803 Latitude, 5 dp (~1 m). Precise location. sensitive-personal 30 days, then aggregate to geohash-5
    payload.lon double no 50.10770 Longitude, 5 dp. Precise location. sensitive-personal 30 days, then aggregate to geohash-5
    payload.speed_kmh double no 15.3 Instantaneous speed in km/h — when producer_version is 2.4.0. Source of gold.eta_features.rolling_avg_speed_10m. See the warning below. internal 30 days raw
    payload.heading_deg int no 211 Compass heading, 0–359. internal 30 days raw
    payload.accuracy_m double no 17.3 Reported GPS accuracy radius in metres, ~3–18. internal 30 days raw
    producer_version string no 2.4.0 Device firmware version. Always carry this into bronze and always segment drift checks by it — without it, the signature incident is undiagnosable. internal keep with the row

    ⚠ speed_kmh is not always km/h¶

    producer_version 2.4.0 emits speed_kmh in kilometres per hour. producer_version 2.5.0 emits the same field name, same double type — in metres per second. This is the course's signature incident (fixtures/incident_speed_unit/). Schema validation, range checks, freshness, volume and row counts all pass. Only a distribution check segmented by producer_version, or a cross-field reconciliation against distance_km / duration_min from trips, catches it.

    Any model or feature reading speed_kmh must either filter on producer_version or normalise: speed_kmh_true = speed_kmh * 3.6 if producer_version >= "2.5.0" else speed_kmh.

    raw/drivers.csv¶

    Driver dimension. 3,450 rows, fixed — not affected by --scale.

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    driver_id string no DRV-00001 Primary key. personal 7 years after termination
    full_name_en string no Sara Al Shehri Driver's name, Latin transliteration. Synthetic, from an internal name pool. personal 7 years after termination
    city string no RUH Home city, one of RUH, JED, DMM. internal 7 years
    hire_date string (three formats) no Jul 22, 2019 Deliberately inconsistent. Approximately: YYYY-MM-DD 62%, DD/MM/YYYY 24%, Mon DD, YYYY 14%. Cast it in staging (stg_drivers) or every date filter downstream is wrong. internal 7 years
    rating double no 4.78 Rolling rider rating, 3.2–5.0, mean ≈ 4.72. A performance measure about a natural person. personal 24 months
    status string no active One of active (90%), inactive (8.5%), suspended (1.5%). personal 7 years
    national_id_hash string (hex, 32) no 8629a325a33df68a… SHA-256 digest (truncated) of a synthetic national-ID string. A pseudonymised national identifier is still personal data under PDPL, and re-identifiable in combination — treat it as sensitive and never join it into a shared mart. sensitive-personal 7 years, strict access
    phone_hash string (hex, 32) no 6958c7fe7046e5d2… SHA-256 digest of a synthetic phone number. Same reasoning. sensitive-personal 24 months, strict access

    raw/vehicles.csv¶

    Vehicle dimension. 3,864 rows (one per driver plus ~12% spare fleet).

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    vehicle_id string no VEH-00001 Primary key. internal 7 years
    plate_hash string (hex, 32) no fde902de0dae2702… SHA-256 digest of a synthetic plate string. A plate identifies a vehicle, and through the fleet roster a person — pseudonymised, not anonymised. personal 7 years, restricted
    make string no Chevrolet Manufacturer. public indefinite
    model string no Malibu Model. public indefinite
    model_year int no 2023 2018–2026, skewed toward recent years. public indefinite
    capacity int no 4 Passenger seats: 4 (saloon), 7 (SUV), 11 (van). public indefinite
    fuel_type string no petrol One of petrol, diesel, hybrid, electric. Feeds the fleet-emissions angle in the capstone. public indefinite
    city string no RUH Registered operating city. internal 7 years

    raw/zones.csv¶

    Zone dimension: 48 rows over three cities. Real district geography, Arabic and English.

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    zone_id string no Z-RUH-01 Primary key, Z-<CITY>-<NN>. public indefinite
    zone_name_en string no Olaya District name, English. public indefinite
    zone_name_ar string no العليا District name, Arabic. UTF-8. Keep the encoding end to end — a mojibake'd Arabic label is a real, common ingestion bug. public indefinite
    city string no RUH RUH (21 zones), JED (15), DMM (12). public indefinite
    district string no Olaya Municipal district grouping. public indefinite
    centroid_lat double no 24.6908 Zone centroid latitude. Reference geography, not a person's location. public indefinite
    centroid_lon double no 46.6853 Zone centroid longitude. public indefinite

    Zone-to-zone flows are strongly non-uniform by design (gravity model with airport, commute and retail boosts): the busiest pair carries ~500 trips over the shipped 5 days against a median of 24. Uniform flows would make partition-skew and data-skipping lessons meaningless.

    raw/payments_YYYY-MM-DD.csv¶

    One row per trip that generated a charge (~96% of trips).

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    payment_id string no PAY-20260601-0000000 Primary key. internal 7 years
    trip_id string no TRP-20260601-0000000 FK → trips.trip_id. Every row references a real trip in the same day's file. internal 7 years
    amount_sar double no 12.01 Amount charged, SAR. Must reconcile with trips.fare_sar — that reconciliation is a Module 6 quality check. internal 7 years (financial record)
    method string no mada Same domain as trips.payment_type. internal 7 years
    status string no settled settled (~95.5%), pending (~2.8%), failed (~1.1%), refunded (~0.6%). internal 7 years
    settled_ts timestamp (UTC) no 2026-06-01 20:08:15 When the payment settled. Can fall on the day after the trip — this is the mechanism behind late-arriving trips and the reason silver.trips needs a lookback window. internal 7 years

    raw/weather_hourly.csv¶

    Hourly weather per city. 72 rows/day (3 cities × 24 hours), independent of --scale.

    column type nullable example business meaning PDPL retention
    city string no DMM RUH, JED, DMM. public indefinite
    hour_ts timestamp (UTC) no 2026-05-31 21:00:00 Hour bucket, UTC. Note that 2026-05-31 21:00:00Z is local midnight on 2026-06-01 — the file starts three hours before the first local day, which is exactly the timezone trap Module 3 drills. public indefinite
    temp_c double no 30.0 Temperature, °C. Peaks ~15:00 local, troughs ~05:00 local; clamped to a plausible KSA June envelope of 20–48. Observed in the shipped sample: 22.2–48.0. public indefinite
    condition string no clear One of clear, cloudy, haze, dust, sandstorm, rain, fog. Conditions persist for several hours (weather states, not per-hour dice), so a dust event lasts a shift, not an hour. Drives surge (up to +0.42 in a sandstorm) and reduces speed (down to ×0.74). Feeds gold.eta_features.weather_condition. The shipped 5-day sample contains clear 207h, dust 67h, cloudy 48h, haze 34h, rain 4h — sandstorm and fog are rare enough that a 5-day window may not contain one; regenerate more days if a lab needs them. public indefinite
    visibility_km double no 10.0 Visibility, 0.2–10.0 km. Falls sharply in sandstorm and fog. Observed in the shipped sample: 1.9–10.0. public indefinite

    raw/_MANIFEST.json¶

    Not a data file — the reproducibility record. Top-level keys:

    key meaning
    seed, args everything needed to reproduce the run exactly
    generator_version bump this if the generator's output changes semantically
    files[] per file: path, kind, rows, bytes, sha256
    documented_defect_rates the rates the labs are written against
    observed_defect_counts what this particular run actually produced
    timezone, currency, synthetic, personal_data provenance

    generators/verify_sample.py compares a directory against this file. See README.md#verifying-a-regeneration.

    fixtures/¶

    Each fixture reuses the schema of the file it derives from, so no new dictionary entries are needed. Full detail, including the expected detection signal, is in each folder's README.md.

    fixture schema lab what is wrong
    corrections/trips_corrections_2026-06-03.csv trips 4 4 rows that must UPDATE, 1 that must INSERT — the MERGE idempotency drill
    late_trips/trips_late_2026-06-02.csv trips 3 120 never-seen trips arriving 2 days late + 6 identical re-sends
    late_pings/gps_late_2026-06-04.ndjson gps_events + _note_delay_seconds 5 520 shuffled, late events straddling a 3-minute watermark
    dirty_batch/trips_2026-06-05_dirty.csv trips 6 40 null keys, 8 duplicate keys, 120 out-of-range fares, 25 bad city codes, 15 reversed timestamps
    currency_shift/trips_2026-06-05_currency_shift.csv trips 6 JED fares ×100 (SAR → halalas), every row individually valid
    erasure_request/pdpl_erasure_2026-06-10.json request document 4 7 rider_ids to erase, 29 rows in the shipped sample
    incident_speed_unit/gps_2026-06-05_v250.ndjson.gz gps_events 5 / 6 / 8 producer_version 2.5.0, speed_kmh carrying m/s

    late_pings/*.ndjson carries one extra key, _note_delay_seconds, stating how late each event is. It is instructional metadata, not part of the contract — strip it before producing to Kafka (emit_gps_stream.py does this automatically).

    Medallion columns added downstream¶

    Not present in raw/ — added by the ingest layer and listed here so the contract is complete.

    column added at type meaning
    _ingested_at bronze timestamp Processing time — when the row landed. Never confuse with pickup_ts / ts (event time).
    _source_file bronze string The file this row came from. The first thing you need in an incident.
    _batch_id bronze string Ingest run identifier; makes a batch retractable.

    CONVENTIONS.md defines the bronze, silver and gold table names these feed.
