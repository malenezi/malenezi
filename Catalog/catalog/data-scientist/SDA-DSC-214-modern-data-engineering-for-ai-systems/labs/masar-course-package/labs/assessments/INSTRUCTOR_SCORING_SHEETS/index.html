    INSTRUCTOR SCORING SHEETS — SDA-DSC-214¶

    Printable, one set per participant. Modern Data Engineering for AI Systems · SDAIA Academy

    How to use. Print §1–§6 double-sided as one four-page pack per participant. Fill §1 rolling through the week (it is the fastest way to spot a participant who will not make M-D). Fill §2 at the end of Days 3, 4 and 5. Fill §3 after the quiz. Transfer the capstone total from the RUBRIC.md worksheet into §4. §5 is the arithmetic. §6 is the badge recommendation and the participant-facing feedback.

    Weightings and thresholds are defined in EVALUATION_PLAN.md; this sheet only applies them.

    Scale note. Figures quoted in this document assume the full-scale generator run (--scale 1.0); the shipped sample is --scale 0.15 — 7,230 trips/day over 5 days (36,150 trips), 873,763 GPS pings, 3,450 drivers, 3,864 vehicles, 48 zones. Grains, ratios and file-count reductions hold at either scale; absolute row counts do not.

    §1 — Lab completion checklist (30% of the course grade)¶

    Eight labs, 3.75 points each. Score each lab C = complete (3.75) · P = partial (1.9) · N = not submitted (0), against that lab's own acceptance criteria. Labs are formative: the intent is coverage, not ranking. Mark honestly anyway — §1 is the early-warning system for the capstone.
