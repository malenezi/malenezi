    Shipped sample vs full scale¶

    The committed data/raw/ is generated at --scale 0.15, verified against the file system:

    shipped (--scale 0.15) lab spec (--scale 1.0)
    trips 7,230/day × 5 days = 36,150 48,200/day × 30 days
    GPS pings 873,763 across 5 gzipped files ~1.16 M/day
    drivers / vehicles / zones 3,450 / 3,864 / 48 identical — these do not scale

    Every document quoting full-scale row counts now carries this sentence near the top:

    Scale note. Figures quoted in this document assume the full-scale generator run (--scale 1.0); the shipped sample is --scale 0.15 — 7,230 trips/day over 5 days (36,150 trips), 873,763 GPS pings, 3,450 drivers, 3,864 vehicles, 48 zones. Grains, ratios and file-count reductions hold at either scale; absolute row counts do not.

    LAB_00 is the exception: it verifies what is on disk, so it now quotes the shipped sample and carries the inverse note.

But build_zones() in data/generators/generate_masar.py emits a hard-coded catalogue of 48 zones at every --scale, and zones.csv is 48 rows. A cohort running the shipped generator will materialise 48 Redis keys, not 412 LAB_08 acceptance, INSTRUCTOR_SCORING_SHEETS, BENCHMARKS_MASTER, CAPSTONE checklist Owner decision, not an editing call: either extend CITY_ZONES to a 412-zone catalogue, or restate the canonical figure as 48.
